JUDGMENT AND ACCOUNTABILITY

No model or agent can:

- approve its own production promotion;
- expand its own permissions;
- accept organizational risk;
- authorize a financial transfer;
- waive a failed security gate;
- waive a failed privacy gate.

Named humans continue to be responsible for those decisions.

CRI's maximum level of autonomy is only for narrow, reversible,
Actions that have a low blast radius and can be independently verified.

Beneficiary data security is more important than convenience - or "maximum" - data.
automation.


REVIEW

A quarterly IT Governance review is conducted by the Director of IT.

The review covers new AI systems, incidents, model / tool changes, security.
Findings, evaluation drift, cost, regulatory changes and policy exceptions.

Severe AI incident – Out of cycle review.
